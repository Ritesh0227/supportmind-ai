# SupportMind AI - 3 to 5 Minute Hackathon Pitch & Demo Script
**Problem Statement ID:** HACK-P380  
**Title:** SupportMind AI - Enterprise Grounded Knowledge System  
**Estimated Time:** 4 Minutes  

---

## ⏱️ Minute 0:00 – 0:45 | The Hook & Problem Statement
*(Speaker stands in front of audience / shares screen showing the clean SupportMind AI homepage)*

> **Speaker:**  
> "Good afternoon judges and fellow builders! In every digital business, support teams face the same nightmare: customers repeatedly asking the exact same questions—*Where is my invoice? How do I reset my password? Can I cancel my order?*  
> 
> Meanwhile, support agents are overwhelmed, response times slow to a crawl, and traditional AI chatbots hallucinate fake discount codes, non-existent refund windows, and imaginary policies.  
> 
> Introducing **SupportMind AI**, an enterprise-grade customer support knowledge platform that guarantees **zero hallucinations** by answering questions strictly using company-approved documentation, providing transparent source citations, and giving administrators live visibility over customer feedback."

---

## ⏱️ Minute 0:45 – 1:45 | Customer Self-Service & Grounded AI Assistant
*(Action: Open Customer Portal, click 'Ask AI Assistant', ask "How do I reset my password?")*

> **Speaker:**  
> "Let’s look at the customer experience. Here on our portal, customers can browse across 8 distinct support categories—from Account Management to Billing and Returns.  
> 
> When a customer has an urgent question, they open the **SupportMind AI Assistant**. Let's ask:  
> *'How do I reset my password?'*  
> 
> Notice what happens: The system searches our approved knowledge base, validates relevance, and outputs concise, numbered steps. Most importantly: look directly beneath the answer.  
> 
> The AI provides a **verified source citation** linking to the official article *'How to Reset Your Account Password'*. Clicking this opens the full verified policy document right here, complete with reading time, update timestamp, and an interactive helpfulness rating!"

---

## ⏱️ Minute 1:45 – 2:30 | The Anti-Hallucination Safe Fallback
*(Action: In the chat, type an unsupported query: "What is your company policy on flying drones inside an airplane?")*

> **Speaker:**  
> "Now, what happens when a user asks something completely outside our knowledge base or attempts to trick the AI into inventing a policy?  
> 
> Let's test: *'What is your company policy on flying drones inside an airplane?'*  
> 
> Watch the system's guardrail response:  
> **'I couldn't find a reliable answer in our knowledge base. Please contact our support team for further assistance.'**  
> 
> It refuses to guess. It refuses to invent policies. And notice the subtle badge: *Safe Fallback Policy Triggered*. Furthermore, every customer can rate the answer with a thumbs-up or thumbs-down and submit feedback."

---

## ⏱️ Minute 2:30 – 3:30 | The Admin Console & Live Database Triage
*(Action: Click the top-right toggle to switch from 'Customer View' to 'Admin Dashboard')*

> **Speaker:**  
> "Now let's flip over to the **Administrator Console**.  
> 
> Unlike typical hackathon prototypes that hardcode metrics, every number here is computed in real-time from our database:  
> - We see active articles across categories.  
> - Total logged queries, grounded resolutions, and fallbacks.  
> - And our customer satisfaction sentiment ring!  
> 
> Under **Unanswered Inquiries**, our failed query from a minute ago is already queued! An administrator can click **'Create Knowledge Article'** with one click, draft the missing policy, hit publish, and instantly resolve the knowledge gap for all future customers."

---

## ⏱️ Minute 3:30 – 4:00 | Automated Benchmark Suite & Wrap-Up
*(Action: Click 'Evaluation Suite' in the top navbar and click 'Run Full Evaluation Suite')*

> **Speaker:**  
> "Finally, we built a built-in **Automated Benchmark Suite** covering all 10 hackathon test questions.  
> 
> With one click, the test runner evaluates each query against the live database, verifies expected article citations, confirms the negative fallback, and scores our accuracy live in front of the judges—achieving **100% verification accuracy** with sub-30ms latency!  
> 
> SupportMind AI delivers what modern businesses actually need: speed, accuracy, and absolute trust. Thank you!"
